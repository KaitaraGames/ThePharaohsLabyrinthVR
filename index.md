# Pharaoh's Labyrinth Privacy Policy

Last updated: Nov 2, 2026

## About this policy

Pharaoh's Labyrinth is a multiplayer VR game for Meta Quest, developed by Kaitara Games ("we", "us"). This policy explains what player data the game collects through the Meta Horizon platform and Photon networking, how that data is used, and how you can ask us to delete it. It applies only to Pharaoh's Labyrinth.

## Data we collect

The game only accesses the data listed below. We do not collect your real name, email address, payment information, location, voice recordings, or any data from outside the game.

| Data | Source | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| User ID | Meta Horizon platform | The unique ID Meta assigns to your account |
| User profile | Meta Horizon platform | Your Meta username (display name) |
| Avatars | Meta Horizon platform | Your Meta Avatar's appearance data |
| Friends | Meta Horizon platform | Your list of Meta friends |
| Invites | Meta Horizon platform | Invitations you send or receive to join a game session |
| Network data | Photon (PUN 2) | Your IP address and in-game state (position, movement, actions) while connected to a multiplayer session |

## How we use your data

We use your data only to run the game's features. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or build profiles about you.

- **User ID:** to confirm you own the game (Meta entitlement check), to load your avatar, and to tell players apart in a multiplayer session.
- **User profile (username):** shown above your character as your in-game name, so other players in your session can see who you are.
- **Avatars:** to render your Meta Avatar in the game, visible to you and the other players in your session.
- **Friends:** to let you find Meta friends and invite them to your session.
- **Invites:** to send and receive invitations so friends can join you in a game.
- **Network data (Photon):** to connect you to other players and keep everyone's game in sync during multiplayer play.

## Who we share it with

Your username, avatar, and in-game actions are visible to the other players in your multiplayer session. Beyond that, data is handled only by these service providers:

- **Meta Platforms, Inc.:** provides your user ID, username, avatar, friends, and invites through the Meta Horizon platform. Meta's handling of your data is covered by the [Meta Privacy Policy](https://www.meta.com/legal/privacy-policy/).
- **Exit Games GmbH (Photon PUN 2):** runs the multiplayer servers that relay game data between players. Photon processes your IP address and in-game state to deliver this service. See the [Photon Privacy Policy](https://www.photonengine.com/privacy).

We do not share your data with anyone else, except where required by law.

## How long we keep it

We do not store your data on our own servers. Your user ID, username, avatar, friends, and invite data are read from the Meta platform while you play and are not saved after you leave the game. Photon handles network data only for the duration of your multiplayer session, under its own retention terms.

## Children

Pharaoh's Labyrinth is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has used the game, contact us and we will delete any related data.
